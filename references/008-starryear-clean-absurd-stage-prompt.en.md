# Starryear-Clean-Absurd-Stage Full Prompt

Treat one user-uploaded photograph as immutable reality evidence and the sole content source for three generated cells. Create one vertical 2:3 true equal 2×2 clean absurd quartet: Evidence top left, Scale Slip top right, Carrier Reversal bottom left, and Impossible Closure bottom right. Do not make an ordinary collage, four filters, or four unrelated spectacles.

## 1. Internal observation and shared proposition

Accept exactly one photograph the user is authorized to use. Internally lock the primary subjects, meaningful count, identity contours, action/gaze, one support or contact relation, one scale cue, decisive object, light, 1–2 dominant colors, and one low-saturation color suitable for broad background fields. Do not output the analysis.

Write one `ABSURD SENTENCE` using source nouns and one impossible verb. Panels 2–4 must develop the same sentence in three steps. Choose one source person, animal, or object as the returning actor; keep its identity anchors while changing scale, position, and carrying relation.

## 2. Four-cell structure

### Panel 1 | Evidence (top left)

Use actual source pixels. Permit only proportional scaling, conservative cropping, or source-colored padding. Do not redraw, beautify faces, recolor, filter, remove, replace, outpaint, or generate missing scenery. Cropping must preserve subjects, action, the key relation, and meaningful count.

### Panel 2 | Scale Slip (top right)

Introduce the first clear scale contradiction from the absurd sentence. Use one giant/miniature relation and retain at least two scale cues. Concentrate actors slightly below center or to one side within a quiet field. Ordinary forced perspective is not enough.

### Panel 3 | Carrier Reversal (bottom left)

Make the object that was ridden, watched, used, or supported carry the subject instead; alternatively turn a body, garment, vehicle, tabletop, or architectural edge into a stage for miniature actors. Continue the same returning actor and impossible law from Panel 2.

### Panel 4 | Impossible Closure (bottom right)

Close the relation into a causal loop: a giant supports the miniature actor who made it, the road carries the person driving it, or an observer is watched by a miniature version of itself. Add no new symbol or second story. The conclusion is more impossible than Panel 3 but remains instantly readable.

## 3. Negative space in every generated cell

Each of Panels 2–4 must contain 45–70% continuous perceptual negative space; default to about 58%. Negative space is one stable, nearly flat source-colored field—not background filled with blurred cities, buildings, forests, trees, clouds, smoke, furniture, crowds, grain, or ornament.

Use at most three actor groups per cell; actors occupy roughly 25–50% of the cell. Keep only one low ground line, narrow plinth, or contact shadow. Concentrate the action and preserve complete breathing room. Generated cells may share related field colors, but their composition, scale, and carrying relation must differ clearly.

## 4. Physical credibility and material

Strangeness comes from relation, not accumulation. Use contact points, weight, occlusion, perspective, cast shadows, material continuity, and gaze to make the impossible credible. Default to deadpan, ceremonial, slightly darkly comic matte stage photography or restrained papier-mâché puppet theatre. Figures may appear mildly handcrafted, but faces, hair, clothing, and posture remain recognizable.

Use one primary material and at most one supporting texture. Derive color from the source: 1–2 dominant colors, one structural dark, and at most one small accent. Background saturation may be lowered. Reject generic beige presets, rainbow or neon cosmos, strong glow, plastic shine, heavy vignette, dirty parchment, dense grain, and splatter.

## 5. Canvas, assembly, and text

The master is vertical 2:3, preferably 3072×4608 and never below 2048×3072. Four cells are equal and individually vertical 2:3. Use a 2–6 px internal divider sampled from a subdued structural source color. No outer frame, rounded cards, center badge, fifth panel, or decorative band.

Generate Panels 2–4 separately, then assemble them deterministically with the original photograph so Panel 1 is never generated. By default prohibit all text, numbers, titles, dates, logos, signatures, watermarks, UI, and pseudo-lettering.

## 6. Exclusions and output

Exclude complex cities, building clusters, forests, crowds, airborne objects, random collage, floating stickers, unsupported eyes/moons/doors/birds/fish/planets/clocks/portals; blood, wounds, organs, dismemberment, horror faces, displaced features, extra limbs, or body melting; repeated compositions, filled cells, four filters, unrelated posters, or redrawn Evidence.

Return only one RGB/sRGB PNG. Do not return analysis, alternatives, or prompt explanation. Review once: Panel 1 uses real pixels; Panels 2–4 develop one absurd sentence and clearly escalate; every generated cell has about 45–70% negative space; subjects remain source-traceable; each cell has one focal relation; contact and weight are credible; and no extra scenery, text, or anatomy artifact appears. Regenerate only a clearly failed cell once.
